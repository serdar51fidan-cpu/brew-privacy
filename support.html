Support

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Contact Us

For any question, problem, or request about brew, write to us at: support.brew@gmail.com Please tell us the phone number your account uses and, where it helps, what you were doing when the problem happened. We aim to respond within a few days.

Signing In

brew uses your phone number to sign in. There is no password and no email address to remember. When you enter your number we send a six digit code by SMS, and entering that code signs you in. If the code does not arrive, check that the number and country code are correct, wait for the countdown on the resend button, then request a new code. Codes are limited to a few per day for each number, so if you have requested several in a row you may need to wait before trying again. If you change your phone number, contact us before you give up the old one. Your account is tied to that number.

Reporting Someone

Open the profile of the person you want to report, select Report, and choose the reason that applies. You can add a description of what happened. You can also remove the match, which permanently ends contact between the two accounts. This takes effect immediately and cannot be undone by the other person. Reports about child safety are reviewed ahead of everything else. See our Child Safety Standards.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time, from Settings. Deletion removes your profile, your photos, and your matches, and it cannot be undone. If you sign up again later with the same phone number, you start from an empty profile. Nothing from the deleted account comes back.

Your Data

What we collect, why, and how long we keep it is set out in our Privacy Policy. To request a copy of your data, or to ask us to correct or delete something, write to support.brew@gmail.com from a message that lets us confirm the account is yours.

Safety

brew is for adults aged 18 and over. Treat the people you meet here with the same caution you would anywhere else online. Do not send money, do not share financial details, and meet in public places. If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first, then report the account to us.
